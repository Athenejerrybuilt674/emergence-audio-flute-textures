## ✨ Key Features

- **Multi-Layer Synthesis**: Blend up to eight layers of flute tones. Each layer has its own volume, pan, and filter controls. Create pads, drones, or evolving beds.
- **Microtonal Tuning**: Play in alternate tuning systems (like just intonation or Pythagorean tuning). Map any MIDI note to your desired pitch. Perfect for experimental composers.
- **MIDI MPE Support**: Connect a MPE-compatible controller (e.g., Roli Seaboard, Sensel Morph, or a standard MIDI keyboard with MPE software). The software responds to pitch bend, aftertouch, and channel pressure per note for expressive performance.
- **High-Fidelity WAV Rendering**: Export any live performance or moment directly to a 24-bit WAV file. Your soundscapes are saved losslessly—ideal for studio recordings or sound design.
- **Presets**: Start from twenty premade ambient, cinematic, and world-instrument-inspired textures. Tweak to your own sound.
- **Automation**: Automate volume, filter cutoff, and mix depth with external MIDI controllers or your DAW's automation lanes.

## 🎛️ How to Use

### Basic Workflow
1. After opening the app, you'll see a main interface with **Layer 1** through **Layer 4** (click + to add more).
2. Click a layer to see its **Waveform** (sine, saw, square) and **LFO** options for movement.
3. For microtonal tuning, go to **Tools > Microtone Editor**. Adjust tuning cents per note in your scale. You can load or save presets.
4. To capture a performance, click **Render** and choose where to save your stereo 24-bit WAV.

### Tips
- Stack two layers with different LFO rates for evolving textures over time.
- Use microtonal scales to create melodies that feel "outside" of standard western tuning—perfect for atmospheric works.
- Connect a MPE controller and set per-note pedal or tilt to trigger LFO depth.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

- **Software doesn't open**: Ensure your Windows is updated. Reboot and try launching as administrator.
- **No sound**: Check your audio output device in Settings. Try switching from standard audio to ASIO (press Ctrl+, for preferences).
- **MIDI not recognized**: Plug in controller before launching software. Go to MIDI menu—select your entry.
- **WAV export fails**: Free some disk space. Ensure file name is under 64 characters.
